# A staffed, permanent precinct

Open **`/Game/Maps/L_Precinct`** in the main `HomicideUnit.uproject`. It is the editor startup map. The game still starts at its desk menu and follows the usual shift flow.

The canonical station assets are `Content/Maps/L_Precinct.umap`, `L_Briefing.umap` and `L_Lobby.umap`. They are saved assets in git, not temporary versions in a Claude/Codex scratch project. `Content/Maps/StationMaps.json` records their revision and SHA-256 hashes.

## Background staff

`UHUPrecinctLife` supplies five uniformed officers (desk sergeant, two bullpen workers, two corridor walkers) and a civilian waiting at reception. It uses EA-07's `HUPeople` cast and `UHUPersonAnimation`, including their existing navy garments, patrol caps and badges. The dedicated uniform/footwear art remains part of the character work; this feature doesn't build substitute full-body figures.

None is a gameplay pawn, suspect or interactable. They have no collision, prompts, dialogue, case data or tasks. Walkers pause at each end of their corridor and yield near detectives. They never enter the squad's personal offices or use its computers, booking counter or case board.

The ceiling fan turns continuously. Two spatial desk phones ring briefly, then stop on their own; subsequent calls are 39–61 seconds apart. Fan and phone sounds attenuate locally and are muffled through walls. There is no global alarm, UI notification or response the squad must make. These are local cosmetics on each rendering machine; dedicated servers don't create them.

`AHUPrecinctLifeLayout` is saved in `L_Precinct` as **Precinct staff / saved positions, phones and fan**. Its editable `Staff`, `FanPosition`, `PhonePositions` and `bEnabled` properties are the authored placement data. Worlds without that actor retain the default layout for compatibility. `-HUNoStaff` disables the effect for comparison.

## Saving and checking maps

The station builders and their child actors have the `HU_SavedStationLayout` tag. Briefing, motor-pool and cruiser startup code respects their saved construction geometry instead of replacing it with a different compiled layout. Untagged legacy maps still get the old automatic upgrade behavior.

Only explicitly running this refresh script regenerates the canonical station geometry from current C++:

```
UnrealEditor.exe HomicideUnit.uproject -ExecutePythonScript=Tools/RefreshStationMaps.py -unattended
AI\python\python.exe -I Tools\CheckStationMaps.py
```

Build first. The refresh keeps existing actors, player starts and gameplay settings, saves all three maps, reopens them to verify the revision and saved staff layout, and records their hashes. Previous `.umap` files are backed up to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\HomicideUnit\MapBackups\<timestamp>`. `Tools/CreateLevels.py` remains missing-level creation only; it doesn't replace an existing map.

## Validation

`-HUStaffTest -HUNoVoice -HUNoBrain` on `L_Precinct` logs a pass after 23 seconds of game time when six real models and animation instances exist, both walkers have progressed, the fan has turned, a phone event has occurred, and every person has no collision or interaction interface. Allow extra time for MetaHuman loading. Screenshot review uses the existing `-HUShots` tour.

The original phone and fan WAVs are generated by `Tools/MakePrecinctLifeAudio.py` and imported by `Tools/ImportPrecinctLifeAudio.py`. The two assets live under `/Game/Art/Audio/PrecinctLife`, already included by the package's always-cooked `/Game/Art` directory. No downloads, voice server or online service are used.
