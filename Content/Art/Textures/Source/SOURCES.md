# PBR texture sources

The production PBR maps in this folder come from ambientCG and are licensed CC0 1.0. The ambientCG license allows commercial use and redistribution without attribution. We retain the asset IDs here for provenance.

| Unreal map prefix | ambientCG asset | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| `M_Plaster` | [Plaster 001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001) | Precinct and building walls |
| `M_Linoleum` | [Tiles 139](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles139) | Worn cream commercial floor tile |
| `M_Walnut` | [Wood 051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood051) | Dark desk veneer and wood trim |
| `M_ComputerPlastic` | [Plastic 004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plastic004) | Beige CRT computer shells and peripherals |
| `M_ManilaFolder` | [Paper 001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Paper001) | Tinted paper case folders |
| `M_VictimWool` | [Fabric 012](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric012) | Victim clothing |

Each material uses the asset's 2K JPG color, DirectX normal, and roughness maps. Displacement and opacity maps are not needed by the current geometry.
